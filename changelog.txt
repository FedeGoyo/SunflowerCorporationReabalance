Below is a list of the changes from the original mod. I only mention changes that the player can notice, cause there are other changes that I made but are more from the codding aspect and players won't notice them, such as adding buffs and toggles to make other things work.

Arc Transformer
- Reduced cost: 20000 -> 10000 (80 -> 36 Steel, 30 -> 22 hypercoils, 20 -> 0 tri-Steel, 2 -> 1 processor)
- Increased energy consumed per arc energy conversion: 1000 -> 2000.
- Increased arc energy converted: 1 -> 1000.
- Increased interval of conversions: 0.5s -> 1.5s.
- Increased maximum power storage: 10000 -> 12000.
- Increased maximum arc energy storage: 8 -> 4000 (in practice it was reduced).
- Changed batteries and arc energy graphics.
- Removed stats (Arc energy conversion rate and arc energy convertion ime).
- Added stats (Arc energy generation, arc energy capacity, power usage continous).
Explanation: The changes were made to balance Arc Emitters. Now it is cheaper, takes longer to to fully charge and stores more energy for easier logistic.

Arc Emitter
- Reduced cost: 26000 -> 15000 (80 -> 36 Steel, 20 -> 0 coils, 40 -> 22 hypercoils, 50 -> 0 tri-Steel, 0 -> 3 processors).
- Added manual targeting.
- Increased minimum resource used: 5 -> 4000 arc energy (in practice it was reduced).
- Removed fire interval : 2 -> 0 (in practice, it increased to once every 6s).
- Now it can only shoot if all active adjacent Arc Transformers are at full arc energy capacity.
- Reduced hit interval: 0.1s -> 1/30s (speed up).
- Increased duration: 0.5s -> 1s (from 5 ticks to 30 ticks of damage).
- Reduced damage per tick (before scaling): 8000 -> 125.
- Added a separated damage per tick for structure: 41.
- Reduced damage scaling with arc energy: squared -> linear.
- Increased arc energy needed to scale: 2 -> 1000 (in practice it was reduced).
- Now damage is affected by ElectronBuff from Electron Nebulas.
- Changed normal damage with explosive damage: 4m radius, 0.5s expansion. Uses only 1 slice so there is no wasted damage.
- Add fire chance: 10% every 20 damage.
- Add damage reduction over distance: 20%.
- Every tune it shots, it creates a 7m radius EMP effect that drains 100 batteries.
- Added stats and replaced some others with more appropiated (fire chance, damage arc near, damage arc far, damage area, arc energy consumed).
- No longer instantaneously destroys shields.
Explanation: Made it cheaper, more unique, easier to use and predict, more effective but also not so ridicusly overpowered. You will notice that previously you could instantaneously obliterate 5 parts/shields every time it shot, as it dealed a ridiculous amount of damage divided in only 5 ticks. Now, it can destroy much more stuff is they are weak but also or much less if they are strong, as not it deals a high amount of damage divided in 30 ticks, which is also treated as an explosive damage to prevent wasting in overkill.
Compared to Deck Cannons, it takes a lot of space, complex logistic as it drains energy, attack slower and is weaker against armor and doesn't enjoy the penetration benefit against normal parts. However, it is better against shields, has much larger range and is hitcan (can't miss or be countered by point defense and flak cannons).

Heavy Ion Blaster
- Reduced cost: 20000 -> 10000 (20 -> 14 coils, 40 -> 22 hypercoils, 1 -> 0 diamonds)

- Fixed and increased arc spawn of scatter: -45d to +0 -> -60d to +60d.
- Reduced lifetime of scatter projectiles: 0.5s to 1.5s -> 0.4s to 1.2s.
- Replaced the explosion of scatter projectiles when lifetime ended with penetration while alive: 3m radius -> 1000 penetration.
- Damage doesn't scale with penetration (the idea is infinite penetration, the limit is based on their lifetime and speed).
- Can't penetrate shields.
- Now damage is affected by ElectronBuff from Electron Nebulas.
- Reduced damage of scatter projectiles: 10000 explosion -> 250 penetration (no structure).
- Reduced damage of scatter projectiles: 6000 explosion -> 125 penetration (structure only).
- Removed impulse of scatter projectiles: 10 every 1000 damage -> 0.
- Added fire chance to scatter projectiles: 10%.
- Added crew stun to scatter projectiles: 0.5s in 1m (doesn't stack).

Explanation: Previously, the damage they deal were extremely high, almost guaranting to destroy the part where the scatter projectiles bursted. Now, the damage is much lower but it penetrates, causing damage in larger area while also stunning crew and fire stuff.
